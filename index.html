Cooperative human-object interaction with heterogeneous teams

One policy.
Many bodies.

MorphHOI is a single decentralized policy that lets humanoids of different heights, limbs and joints carry objects together.

Any body.

New skills.
Boxes, paths and stairs down plug in with a quick fine-tune, and multiple skills live side by side in one policy.

Every body is different.

Heights, masses, limb lengths and joint layouts all vary. Each agent is told what its own body and its teammates' bodies look like, so the same network adapts its grip, gait and timing to the team it is in.

Any team size.

Every agent runs the same decentralized policy and attends to its teammates, so a team grows without retraining. Trained only with two to four agents, it generalizes to teams of eight to sixteen it never trained with.

New skills.
Just plug them in.

Each skill is a token with a small LoRA on the frozen policy, switched on per agent. Adding one leaves the others untouched: teams carry boxes, follow paths, go down stairs and turn a table through a gap.

Carry a box. Added skill.
Follow a path. Added skill.
Down the stairs. Added skill.
Turn it, fit it through. Path and pose skill: the planned table poses turn it lengthwise before the gap.

Teammates come and go.

The team is read again at every step, so an agent can join a carry already under way, or walk away from one, and the others re-space around the table and carry on.

Transfer to robots.

The same policy and skills drive humanoid robots, each with its own joints, limits and mass: eight around a table, two with a long box, two down the stairs, four through a gap.
